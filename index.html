Questions teens ask us

Who can join Funngro?

Funngro is built for young people who want to freelance. Check the app for the latest age and eligibility rules before you sign up.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Start with projects that match what you already know, then build your profile and portfolio as you finish more work.

How do I get paid?

Payments go straight to the bank account or wallet linked to your Funngro profile.

How much can I earn?

It depends on the projects you take and how well you deliver. The pay is shown on every project before you start.
